Rebuilds `goneat-tools-slim-musl`, `goneat-tools-runner-musl`, and `goneat-tools-runner-glibc` with sfetch **v0.4.14**. ## Upgrade notes

Consumers pinned to `:v0.5.13` (or its digest) should retag to `:v0.5.14` after GA. Verify an image with `cosign verify --certificate-identity https://github.com/fulmenhq/fulmen-toolbox/.github/workflows/release.yml@refs/tags/v0.5.14 --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com <image>@<digest>`, for the index digest or the platform digest you run. Checksum manifests verify with the keys from the repository at the tag; see `docs/security/release-signing.md`.
